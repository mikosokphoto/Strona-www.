Privacy policy — Paediatric DRUGS

Version of 9 November 2026 · Android app pl.pediatrialeki.mobile

The app does not collect or transmit data

Paediatric DRUGS runs entirely on the device: it does not require an account, does not use the internet (it has no network access permission) and contains no ads, analytics or tracking tools. The publisher receives no personal data or health data.

Case data

Weight, age, date of birth, gestational age and body length are used only for the calculations shown on screen. They are not saved — they disappear after choosing “New case” or closing the app. Use fictional data in exercises. Do not enter personal data of real patients, including in the catalogue text fields.

What stays on the device

The app stores only: the selected language, text size, your custom drug catalogue (if you create one) and the catalogue sort order. This data never leaves the device and is not included in Android backups. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it permanently.

Catalogue backup file

A backup file is created only when you choose “Save backup to file” and is saved in the location you pick in the system save dialog. Loading a backup reads only the file you select. What happens to the file afterwards (sharing, deleting) is up to you.

Links to sources

Links to SmPCs, guidelines and publications open in an external browser. From then on, the privacy policy of the visited website applies.

Permissions and recipients

The app does not request any system permissions and does not share data with third parties. It is intended for training medical staff, not for children.

Changes and contact

A new version of this policy will appear in the app and at this address, together with its date.
